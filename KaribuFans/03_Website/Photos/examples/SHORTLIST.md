# Example photos by place type: shortlist

Prepared by the Photo researcher for Kiongozi, 9 October 2026. Nothing in the repository was changed.

## Read this first: what was and was not checked

- This workspace blocks unsplash.com, pexels.com, images.unsplash.com and wordpress.org (proxy egress policy). **No photo page was opened and no image was viewed.** Everything below comes from search-engine results: the page URL, the title, the photographer, and sometimes the licence line and location shown in the search snippet. Every candidate is therefore marked **not opened**.
- **Size is not verified for any candidate.** Unsplash and Pexels originals are usually 4000 px or more wide, but some are portrait or small. Check on the page: the photo must be landscape and at least 2000 px wide.
- **Licence:** where the snippet said "Unsplash License" or "Free to use" on Pexels, that is noted. Unsplash also sells **Unsplash+** photos (paid, NOT allowed). Before downloading, confirm the page shows "Free to use under the Unsplash License" and a plain **Download** button, not "Unsplash+" or a lock icon.
- Before use, every photo still needs a visual check against the brief: no business names, logos, signs, readable labels on bottles or cans, TV channel logos, number plates or faces in focus.

### Direct download links
Not visible, because no page could be opened. The usual patterns, **untested here**, are:
- Unsplash: `https://unsplash.com/photos/<ID>/download?force=true` (ID = the last part of the page URL, for example `2PDJ7BgL0Y0`)
- Pexels: `https://www.pexels.com/download/photo/<number>/` (number = the digits at the end of the page URL)

The safe way is to use the Download button on the page (see "How to download" at the end).

### Consolidation used
- **Bar and lounge:** one "lounge" image covers *Hotel bar* (Matchday) and *nightclub/lounge* when the place is a lounge. *Sports bar* (football on screens), *Bar & live music* and *Nightclub* each have their own image.
- **Restaurant (general)** is a separate image, used for any restaurant that has no cuisine type.
- **Charcoal grill (nyama choma)** and **Brazilian steakhouse** are separate but close. If one has to cover both, use the charcoal grill pick.

## How to download (for Marx)

1. Open the photo's page link from the table above in your browser.
2. Check three things on the page:
   - **Unsplash:** under the photo it must say "Free to use under the Unsplash License". If it says **Unsplash+** or shows a lock, skip it.
   - **Pexels:** the page shows "Free to use" and a **Free download** button.
   - **The photo itself:** it is landscape (wider than tall), and there are no readable names, logos, bottle labels, TV channel logos or clear faces. If there is something small at the edge, note it; Kiongozi's builder can crop or paint it out in `tools/photos.py`.
3. Download the largest size:
   - **Unsplash:** click the arrow beside **Download** and choose **Original size**.
   - **Pexels:** click the arrow beside **Free download** and choose **Original size**.
4. Save the file into the repository at `KaribuFans/03_Website/Photos/examples/` using the file name from the summary table (for example `type-hotel.jpg`). Create the `examples` folder if it does not exist. Keep the originals there untouched; do not put them in `static/img/photos/`, which `photos.py` generates.
5. Write down, for each file: the photographer's name, the page URL, and the licence (Unsplash or Pexels). Send this list to Kiongozi with the files. Each photo needs a credit on the card and in the Photo credits section of the privacy notices.
6. For **Ethiopian** and **Brazilian**, open the search pages listed under those types, choose an overhead food photo that meets step 2, and send its page link along with the file.

Once the files are in place, the builder adds them to `tools/photos.py` (crop, sizes, credit, alt text that describes the scene and never names a business or claims Nairobi), runs `photos.py`, then `build.py`.
