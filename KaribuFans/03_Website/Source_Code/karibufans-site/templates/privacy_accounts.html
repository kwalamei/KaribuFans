Privacy notice

Last updated {{ site.privacy_updated }}

{{ site.name }} is a city guide for Nairobi. You do not need an account to use it. If you choose to sign in, your name, your email address, a few sign-in details and the places you add to your account are kept for you. This notice explains the little information that is involved and who handles it.

Who we are

{{ site.name }} is run from Nairobi, Kenya. For any question about this notice or your information, email {{ site.contact_email }}.

What stays on your own device

To save you repeating yourself, your browser remembers a few choices on your own phone or computer: the area and filters you picked, your search words on each page, the dates and number of guests you entered for a stay, and the places you save. This is kept in your browser's local storage, and you can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. It is not sent to us, with one exception: if you sign in, the places you add to your account are sent to it, as described under Accounts below.

Accounts

An account is optional. Its only purpose is to keep your saved places together, so that you see them on any phone or computer you sign in on.

  • What is kept. Your name, your email address, the display name you choose on the account page, and the places you add to your account (for each place, which place it is, the page of this site it is on, and when you added it). {% if google_on %}When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, your email address, your Google account identifier and the web address of your profile picture with the sign-in service, which stores them. We do not show the picture. {% endif %}{% if email_on %}If you sign in by email, the sign-in service stores the address you type. {% endif %}The sign-in service also keeps when your account was created and the times you signed in.
  • Why. To sign you in, to greet you by name, and to show your saved places. We do not use these details for advertising and we do not sell them or pass them to the businesses listed here.
  • Who provides it. Sign-in and storage are provided by Supabase, a separate company that runs them on our behalf{% if google_on %}, and Google sign-in is provided by Google, whose own privacy policy applies to your Google account{% endif %}. Like any online service, they also process technical details such as your IP address.
  • Where it is kept. Your account and the places saved in it are stored by Supabase in its Central EU region, in Frankfurt, Germany. This means they are kept outside Kenya.
    • {% if email_on %}
  • Signing in by email. When you ask for a sign-in link, the address you typed is sent to the sign-in service straight away, before you have signed in, so that it can send the link. The link itself is sent to you by an email delivery service.
    • {% endif %}
  • No password. {{ site.name }} does not store a password for you. {% if google_on and email_on %}You sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email address.{% elif google_on %}You sign in with your Google account.{% else %}You sign in with a link sent to your email address.{% endif %}
  • Places already saved on your device. Places you saved before signing in are added to your account only if you agree when the account page asks. If you say no, they stay on that device only. Places you save while you are signed in go to your account.
  • In your browser. While you are signed in, the sign-in service keeps a sign-in token in this browser's local storage, together with a copy of your saved places. A short record of your choices also stays there after you sign out (that you kept a place on this device only, or removed a place while offline), labelled with your account identifier, which is a random code and not your name or email address. The code that runs sign-in is loaded from the jsDelivr network when you open the account page, and on the other pages for as long as you are signed in; those pages then also contact the sign-in service to keep your saved places up to date. If you are not signed in, only the account page loads it. When the account page opens and you are not signed in, it also asks the sign-in service which ways of signing in are switched on; that request says nothing about you beyond the technical details mentioned above.
  • Returning you to your page. When you open the account page from another page of this site, your browser notes that page's address in its local storage, so that you can be taken back there after signing in. It is not sent to us. It is used for up to an hour, and it is removed when you move on from the account page after signing in, when you sign out, or the next time you open the account page after the hour has passed. For the moment of the move, a one-time marker is also kept for that tab, so that the page you arrive on can confirm you are signed in; it is removed as soon as that page reads it.
  • Signing out. Signing out removes the sign-in token from this browser, and removes from it the places that are in your account; they stay in your account. Places that were only ever on the device stay on the device.
  • Deleting your account. Email {{ site.contact_email }} from the address you sign in with{% if google_on and not email_on %} (the email address of your Google account){% endif %} and ask us to delete your account. We delete the account, which removes its saved places. You can remove single places yourself at any time on the account page.

What we receive from you

Apart from an account, we receive information only when you choose to message us on WhatsApp, for example to list a business, claim or remove a listing, or ask for a ticket sale alert. The form on this site only prepares the message: it opens WhatsApp with your words written out to our number{% if site.contact_whatsapp %} (+{{ site.contact_whatsapp }}){% endif %}, and you send it yourself. The form does not send or store anything on this site. We then see your WhatsApp number and what you wrote, and we use them only to reply and to do what you asked.

When you message a business from a listing, that message goes straight from you to the business. We do not see it, and we never handle your payment.

Other services this site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it processes technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages.
  • Fonts. The typefaces are served from this site itself, so no font service is contacted.
  • Links out. Directions open Google Maps, ride buttons open Uber or Bolt, booking buttons open WhatsApp, and listings link to each business's own website. Once you leave this site, that service's own privacy policy applies.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this site.

Business listings

A card marked “Directory listing” shows only a business's name and location as published on its own website. If it is your business, you can claim the listing to add your details, or ask us to remove it, from the link on the listing or by email. We act on removal requests promptly.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be told how your personal data is used, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. To exercise any of these, email {{ site.contact_email }}. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

If the site starts to collect anything more, for example when online booking is added, we will update this notice first and change the date at the top.
