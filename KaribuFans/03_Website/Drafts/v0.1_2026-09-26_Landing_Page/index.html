Questions

Is KaribuFans the official tournament website?

No. KaribuFans is an independent Kenyan service. It is not affiliated with CAF, the organising committee or the stadiums. Match tickets are only sold through official channels.

How do payments work?

Guests will pay by M-Pesa or card when they book. Hosts receive their payout after the guest checks in. Online payments are being set up and will open before bookings go live.

How are hosts verified?

Each host shares an ID and photos of the space, and we confirm the location before the listing is published.

Which venues host matches in Nairobi?

Talanta Stadium on Ngong Road and Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani. The final match schedule will be confirmed by the organisers.
