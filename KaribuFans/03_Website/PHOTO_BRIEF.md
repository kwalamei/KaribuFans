# KaribuFans photo brief

One brief for two uses:
1. **Now:** stand-in images made with an AI image generator (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney or similar). They go live labelled "Artist's impression", as the current ones are.
2. **Next week:** the shot list for the Nairobi photo walk. Real photos replace the stand-ins one slot at a time.

You only make **one large master per slot**. The lead turns each master into every size and format the site needs (WebP and AVIF at 640, 1280, 1920 and 2560 px wide, plus phone crops). Do not resize or compress anything yourself.

---

## 1. Master file requirements (every image)

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | Long edge at least 3840 px (4K). Bigger is fine. |
| Shape | As listed per slot (mostly 16:9 landscape) |
| Format | JPEG at maximum quality, or PNG. Camera photos: the camera's highest-quality JPEG (keep the RAW too). |
| Colour | sRGB |
| Must not contain | Watermarks, text, captions, borders, logos, brand names, readable signs or number plates |
| File name | Exactly as listed below, e.g. `home-hero.jpg` |

Put them all in one folder called `karibufans-photos/` and upload the folder (or a zip) into the Claude chat.

## 2. Content rules (from the team brief; these are firm)

- **No logos or brands:** no CAF or tournament marks, no Uber, Bolt or matatu company branding, no bank, beer or telecom adverts in shot.
- **No real business shown as if it were a listing.** A business that has not joined KaribuFans may not have a photo on the site. Shop fronts and restaurant interiors must be generic, or of businesses that have agreed in writing.
- **People:** AI people must be fictional. In real photos, anyone recognisable needs their permission (a signed release is best); otherwise use crowds, silhouettes, backs or motion blur.
- **No prices, dates or fixtures** visible anywhere.
- **Honesty:** AI images stay labelled "Artist's impression" on the site until they are replaced by real photos.

## 3. The look (paste this "style block" in front of every prompt)

> Editorial travel photograph of Nairobi, Kenya, in the style of a premium magazine or a Squarespace template: one clear subject, uncluttered composition, generous negative space, natural light, true-to-life colour, gentle contrast, sharp focus, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35 mm lens, photorealistic. Contemporary, confident and warm, not touristy and not a cliché safari shot. No text, no logos, no brand names, no readable signs, no number plates, no watermark.

**Text space:** headlines sit over the **left half and the bottom third** of every hero. Keep those areas calm (sky, wall, road, shadow), and put the subject in the **right third**. The site darkens the photo behind the text automatically, so do not darken it yourself.

**Phone crop:** on phones the same image is cropped to a tall frame around the middle-right. Keep the main subject inside the central 50% of the width so it survives the crop.

### E. Business photos (supplied by each business when it joins, not stock)

Listed so businesses can be told what to send: **3 to 6 landscape photos**, at least 2400 px wide, the first being their best wide shot. The site shows them at 2:1 on cards and as a one-large, two-small gallery in the details panel. Never use AI images for a real business.

## 6. What the lead does with them

1. Check each against this brief and the brand rules. Anything off-brief is reported back, not used.
2. Make all the sizes and formats (WebP and AVIF, 640/1280/1920/2560 px, phone crops, lazy loading), keeping the pages fast on Kenyan mobile data.
3. Rebuild the design in the Squarespace-style direction around the photos, and send before-and-after screenshots.
4. Keep "Artist's impression" on every AI image until a real photo replaces it.
