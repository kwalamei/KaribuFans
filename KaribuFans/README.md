# KaribuFans Project

Nairobi visitor platform for the June–July 2027 football tournament: stays, food, nightlife, rides and matchday in one website.

## Folder guide
- 00_Admin: business registration, legal and compliance, finance
- 01_Strategy: business model, roadmap, market research
- 02_Brand_and_Design: moodboards, logo, colours and typography, photography, UI mockups
- 03_Website: drafts, source code, hosting and domain, testing
- 04_Modules: Stays, Food, Nightlife, Rides, Matchday, Trip_Planner (each with Specs, Designs, Content)
- 05_Vendors: onboarding, listings, agreements
- 06_Content_and_Translations: EN, FR, AR, PT
- 07_Marketing: social media, partnerships
- 08_Meetings_and_Decisions: decision log and meeting notes

## Naming rule
Start dated files and folders with YYYY-MM-DD, e.g. 2026-09-26_Theme_v1.
