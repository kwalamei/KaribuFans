# Gemini photo brief: KaribuFans v3 (27 September 2026)

Paste the prompt below into Gemini together with the two reference photos (window view at golden hour; expressway at dusk).

```
Create a set of photoreal images for a Nairobi visitor website, matching the style of the two attached reference photos: cinematic, natural light, lush green tree canopy, Nairobi skyline, either warm golden hour ("Day") or deep blue dusk with amber city lights ("Night"). Colour palette: canopy green, sun gold, night indigo, amber light.

Rules for every image: landscape 16:9, highest resolution available (at least 2400 px wide); no text, no logos, no brand names, no team crests or real kits; no recognisable real people or real businesses; people shown from behind, in silhouette or softly out of focus; leave calm space on one side for headline text.

Make one image for each shot:
1. STAY-01 (Day): hotel balcony with two chairs overlooking the green canopy and skyline at golden hour.
2. STAY-02 (Day): bright, modern hotel room with a window view of trees and city.
3. FOOD-01 (Day): nyama choma platter with kachumbari and ugali on a wooden table outdoors, late afternoon light.
4. FOOD-02 (Night): busy open-air restaurant terrace at dusk, warm string lights, groups of fans in plain coloured shirts.
5. NIGHT-01 (Night): rooftop bar at blue hour with the lit skyline behind.
6. NIGHT-02 (Night): club dance floor with amber and deep blue lights, crowd in soft focus.
7. MOVIES-01 (Night): cinema hall, empty seats lit by the screen's glow.
8. MOVIES-02 (Night): gaming lounge with players on consoles, screens glowing, faces not visible.
9. RIDES-01 (Night): car pulling over for a pickup on a tree-lined Nairobi road at dusk, light trails behind, no company markings.
10. MATCH-01 (Night): fans walking toward a distant floodlit stadium at dusk, stadium soft and in the background.
11. MATCH-02 (Night): outdoor fan zone with a large screen and a crowd seen from behind.
12. TICKETS-01 (Night): hand holding a phone showing a plain e-ticket with a blank QR code, stadium floodlights blurred behind.
```

Notes
- Shot codes match the photo slots on the v3 pages.
- The stadium must stay generic and soft in the background. It is not the real Talanta Stadium, and every stadium image carries an "Artist's impression" label on the site.
- Whether a visible Gemini watermark appears depends on the Gemini plan and tool used; check each image before use.
